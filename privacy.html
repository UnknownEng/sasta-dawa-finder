Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026 (visit and install counts added). Applies to the website sasta-dawa-finder.vercel.app and the Sasta Dawa app for Android and iOS.

Sasta Dawa Finder shows official DRAP medicine prices and cheaper brands with the same formula. We built it to work without accounts, and we keep as little data as we can.

What stays on your phone or computer

  • My Medicines (the medicines you add and how many you take), recently viewed medicines, your language and theme, and reminder times are saved only on your device. We never receive them. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data deletes them.
  • Medicine reminders (app only) are scheduled by your phone. They work without internet and nothing is sent to us.
  • Search runs on your device using the medicine list that ships with the app/site. Your searches are not sent to us.

What is sent to our server

  • Feedback you choose to send (wrong price, missing medicine, suggestion): your message, the medicine or search it is about, your language, the time, and a phone number or email only if you type one in. We use it to fix prices and reply to you, and we delete reports once they are dealt with.
  • Assistant questions in AI mode: when AI answers are switched on, the messages you type in the Dawa Assistant are sent through our server to Anthropic's Claude model to write a reply. We don't store these conversations. Anthropic processes them under its own privacy policy and does not use them to train its models. Don't type personal details such as your name, CNIC or medical history into the assistant.
  • Technical data: like any website, our hosting provider (Vercel) handles your IP address and basic request information to deliver the service and block abuse (for example, limiting how many assistant messages one address can send). We don't use it to identify you.

Visit and install counts

  • Website: we use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views and which pages are popular. It uses no cookies, doesn't follow you to other sites and doesn't store personal information. Search words are not sent.
  • App: the first time you open the app, and at most once a day after that, it loads a small page from our website so we can count installs and daily users. It sends no ID, no medicine list and nothing you typed.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no advertising, no cookies for tracking, no third-party tracking SDKs, and no selling or sharing of data.
  • We don't access your contacts, location, camera or files. "Pharmacies near me" simply opens Google Maps, where Google's policy applies.

Other services

  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • Links to WhatsApp, Google Maps and other sites open those services, which have their own privacy policies.

Medical information

Prices are the maximum retail prices notified by DRAP. Sasta Dawa Finder does not give medical advice, doses or diagnoses. Always ask your doctor or pharmacist before switching medicines. In an emergency call Rescue 1122 or Edhi 115.

Children

The service is meant for adults buying medicines for themselves or their families. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Your choices and contact

To have a feedback report you sent deleted, or for any privacy question, email mavericktech.pk@gmail.com. If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
