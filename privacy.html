This policy explains how Maverick Technologies, Islamabad ("we") handles information in Sasta Dawa Finder: the website sasta-dawa-finder.vercel.app, the Sasta Dawa Finder and Sasta Dawa for Pharmacies apps for Android, the clinic widget and the developer API. We built the service to work without an account and we keep as little data as we can. We never sell your data and we show no advertising.

1. If you use the service without an account

  • Search runs on your device using the medicine list that comes with the site or app. Your searches are not sent to us.
  • My Medicines, recently viewed medicines, language, theme and reminder times are saved only on your device. Reminders (app) are scheduled by your phone.
  • Feedback you choose to send (wrong price, missing medicine, suggestion): your message, the medicine or search, your language, the time, and a phone number or email only if you type one.
  • Dawa Assistant in AI mode: the messages you type are sent through our server to Anthropic's Claude model to write a reply. We do not store these conversations. Do not type your name, CNIC or medical history into the assistant.
  • Visit counts: Vercel Web Analytics counts page views without cookies. The app loads a small page at most once a day so we can count installs and daily users; it sends no ID and nothing you typed.

2. If you create an account

  • Account details: name, email, phone number (optional), account type, plan, and your password stored only as a secure hash (we cannot read it). If you sign in with Google we receive your name, email and Google account ID from Google.
  • Sign-in records: how you signed in (password, Google or the app), when, and a short device label such as "Chrome on Android". We show these in your Profile so you can spot a sign-in that was not you.
  • Terms acceptance: the date you agreed to our Terms and Privacy Policy.
  • Your medicines list, if you choose to sync My Medicines to your account.
  • Payments: plan, amount, method (JazzCash, Easypaisa, bank), transaction ID and the payer phone you enter. We never see or store card numbers or wallet PINs.
  • Pharmacy accounts: shop name, address, area, city, map pin, phone, WhatsApp, hours, stock and prices, offers, profile statistics (views and taps), reservations and delivery orders customers send you, and the refill customer list you keep (customer names, phones and medicines).
  • Clinic, NGO, company and developer accounts: widget settings and usage counts, saved medicine lists and supplier quotes, patient records an NGO adds, company and staff codes, API keys and API usage counts. Claims files uploaded by companies are used to calculate the report and are not stored.

3. Location and photos

  • Location is used only when you tap "use my location", to sort pharmacies by distance. It is used on your device and is not stored on our servers. (Pharmacies' own map pins are public parts of their listings.)
  • Photos of a prescription or medicine strip for the scan feature are read on your device and stay on your device. Only if on-device reading fails and you choose the AI fallback is the photo sent through our server to Anthropic's Claude to read the medicine names; it is not stored.

4. Where your data is stored

Account data is stored with our hosting provider Vercel (Vercel Inc.), on servers in the United States. This means your data is transferred outside Pakistan. Vercel processes it only to provide hosting and storage for us. Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections.

5. Why we use it

  • To run your account and the features you use (listings, reservations, refill reminders, widget, API).
  • To process plan payments and keep accounting records.
  • To keep the service secure: stopping abuse, spotting suspicious sign-ins, and making backups.
  • To fix wrong prices and improve the service, using counts that do not identify you.
  • To contact you about your account or plan (for example a renewal reminder). We do not send marketing without asking.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account data: while your account is open. When you delete your account it is removed from the live service straight away, and from our automatic backups within 14 days. See Delete your account.
  • Feedback reports: until they are dealt with, then deleted.
  • Payment records: where the law requires us to keep a record of a payment, we keep only what is needed (amount, date, method and transaction ID).
  • Automatic backups: one per day, the last 14 kept.
  • Hosting request logs (IP address, time, page): kept by Vercel for a short time for security.

7. Who can see your data

  • Pharmacies: when you reserve a medicine or order delivery, that pharmacy sees the details you send (your name, phone, the medicine and quantity, any note, and your address for delivery). It uses them to serve you and may contact you on WhatsApp or by phone.
  • Visitors see pharmacy listings (name, address, map pin, phone, hours, stock and prices). Nothing else about an account is public.
  • Service providers who help us run the service, only for that purpose: Vercel (hosting and storage), Google (only if you use Google sign-in; Google Fonts loads the fonts), Anthropic (AI assistant and photo fallback), and JazzCash (if you choose to pay online).
  • The law: we share information if Pakistani law or a valid legal order requires it.
  • We do not sell or rent data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

8. How we protect it

HTTPS everywhere, passwords stored as salted hashes, sign-in tokens that can be revoked ("Sign out everywhere"), checks on everything that is uploaded, a 2-step login for our admin panel, and access limited to the people who run the service.

9. Your choices and rights

  • See and download your data: Account → Profile → Download my data (a JSON file).
  • Correct it: Account → Profile, or your pharmacy's "Edit details".
  • Delete it: Account → Profile → Delete account, or ask us on WhatsApp or email.
  • Stop using Google sign-in by setting a password in your Profile; ask us to remove a feedback report; or ask any question about your data.

10. Children

Accounts are for adults (18 or older). The free search is meant for adults buying medicines for themselves or their families. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Medical information

Prices are DRAP maximum retail prices. We do not give medical advice. See the Medical Disclaimer.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. If the change is important, we will also tell you on the site, in the app or by email.
