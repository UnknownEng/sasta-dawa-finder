You can delete your Sasta Dawa Finder account at any time, on the website or in the app. Deleting cannot be undone.

1. Before you delete

  • If you want a copy of your data, go to Account → Profile → Download my data first.
  • There is nothing to cancel: plans never renew automatically. Paid time left on a plan is not refunded (see Refund & Cancellation).

2. How to delete it yourself

  1. Sign in at your account (on the website, or the Account tab in the app).
  2. Open Profile and scroll to Delete account.
  3. Enter your password (if you sign in with Google, type DELETE instead), tick "I understand this cannot be undone" and press Delete my account.

3. Or ask us

If you cannot sign in, message us on WhatsApp +92 339 3202419 or email mavericktech.pk@gmail.com from the phone number or email address of your account, and say "Please delete my Sasta Dawa account". We may ask a question to make sure the request is really from you. We delete the account within 7 days and confirm when it is done.

4. What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, phone, password hash, Google sign-in link and sign-in records.
  • Your synced medicines list.
  • Pharmacy accounts: your listings (they disappear from the site), stock and prices, offers, statistics, reservations and delivery orders, and refill customer lists.
  • Clinic, NGO, company and developer accounts: widget settings, saved lists and quotes, patient records, company and staff codes, API keys and usage.
  • All your sign-in sessions, on every device.

5. How long it takes

  • Your account is removed from the live service immediately when you confirm (or within 7 days if you asked us). Pages saved by browsers or search engines may take a little longer to refresh.
  • Copies inside our automatic daily backups are purged within 14 days, as old backups are deleted.

6. What may be kept

  • Where the law requires us to keep a record of a payment: the amount, date, method and transaction ID only.
  • Counts that do not identify you (for example "a pharmacy profile was viewed 120 times this month").
  • Feedback reports you sent from the site are not linked to your account; ask us if you want one removed.

If you reserved a medicine at a pharmacy, that pharmacy may have your details in its own records (for example WhatsApp chats). Ask the pharmacy directly to delete those.
