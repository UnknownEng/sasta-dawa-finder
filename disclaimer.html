Sasta Dawa Finder is a price information service. It does not give medical advice, diagnosis, doses or treatment. Nothing on the website, in the apps, in the Dawa Assistant or in a scan result replaces advice from a qualified doctor or pharmacist.

1. Prices are DRAP maximum retail prices

  • Prices shown are the maximum retail prices (MRP) notified by DRAP, the Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan. A pharmacy may sell for less, but should not charge more than the MRP.
  • DRAP changes prices from time to time. Our list is updated regularly but may be out of date or contain mistakes. Always check the price printed on the pack.
  • Prices shown by a pharmacy in its listing are set by that pharmacy.
  • If you find a wrong price, please use "Report a wrong price" on the medicine page.

2. "Same formula" does not always mean "right for you"

We list brands with the same active ingredient (salt), strength and form. Such brands are generally meant to work the same way, but:

  • inactive ingredients differ and can matter if you have an allergy or intolerance;
  • some medicines need extra care when switching brands, for example medicines for epilepsy, thyroid, heart rhythm, blood thinning, transplants, and modified-release (SR, XR, CR) tablets;
  • your doctor may have chosen a brand for a reason.

Ask your doctor or pharmacist before switching to another brand. Never stop a medicine or change its dose on your own.

3. Assistant and scan results

The Dawa Assistant and the prescription / strip scan can make mistakes, including reading a name or strength wrongly. Always compare the result with the pack or prescription, and ask your pharmacist if anything is unclear.

4. Pharmacies and availability

Pharmacy details, stock and prices come from the pharmacies themselves. A "Verified" badge means we checked basic details, not that we recommend the pharmacy. Stock can change quickly: call or WhatsApp the pharmacy before you go.

5. No endorsement

Listing a medicine, brand, manufacturer or pharmacy is not a recommendation. We do not accept money to show a medicine higher in results. Sponsored pharmacy listings are always marked "Sponsored".

6. Emergencies

In an emergency, call Rescue 1122 or Edhi 115, or go to the nearest hospital. For a possible poisoning or overdose, get medical help immediately.
