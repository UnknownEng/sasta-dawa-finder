Pharmacy Listing Terms

Last updated 27 Nov 2026

خلاصہ

صرف لائسنس یافتہ فارمیسیاں (DRAP / صوبائی ڈرگ سیل لائسنس) اپنی دکان لسٹ کر سکتی ہیں۔ نام، پتہ، فون، اوقات، اسٹاک اور قیمتیں درست رکھیں اور قیمت DRAP کی حد سے زیادہ نہ ہو۔ ادویات کی تشہیر یا صحت کے دعوے نہ کریں۔ نسخے والی ادویات صرف قانون کے مطابق دیں۔ ریزرویشن کی پابندی کریں اور گاہک کی معلومات صرف اسی کی خدمت کے لیے استعمال کریں۔ غلط معلومات یا قانون کی خلاف ورزی پر ہم لسٹنگ ہٹا سکتے ہیں۔

These terms apply to every pharmacy or medical store listed on Sasta Dawa Finder, on top of our Terms of Service. By adding a listing you confirm that you agree to them and that you are allowed to act for the pharmacy.

1. Licensed pharmacies only

  • Only pharmacies and medical stores with a valid drug sale licence issued under the Drugs Act 1976 and your province's drug rules (or by DRAP where it applies) may be listed.
  • We may ask for a copy of the licence, the owner's or qualified person's details, or a photo of the shop before verifying or at any time later. A listing stays hidden until we verify it.
  • Tell us straight away if your licence is suspended, cancelled or expires. Your listing must be hidden until it is valid again.

2. Accurate information

  • Keep your name, address, map pin, phone, WhatsApp and opening hours correct.
  • Keep your stock list honest: mark medicines "out" when you run out.
  • Prices you show must be real prices at your counter and must not be higher than the DRAP maximum retail price.
  • Staff discounts and offers you publish must be honoured as described.

3. No medicine advertising or health claims

  • Do not use your listing, offer text or messages sent through the service to advertise or promote any medicine or brand, or to make claims that a product cures or treats a condition.
  • Do not publish content that encourages self-medication, misuse, or buying prescription-only medicines without a prescription.
  • General store information (for example "home delivery available" or "10% off for staff of partner companies") is fine.

4. Dispensing the law's way

Reservations and delivery orders do not change your legal duties. Supply prescription-only medicines only against a valid prescription, never supply controlled (narcotic or psychotropic) medicines except as the law allows, and give advice only through a qualified person. You are fully responsible for the medicines you sell and the advice you give.

5. Honour reservations

  • Answer reservations and delivery orders quickly. If you cannot supply a medicine, mark the reservation "Cancelled" and tell the customer.
  • When you mark a reservation "Ready", keep the medicine for the customer for a reasonable time (at least until the end of the next business day unless you agree otherwise).
  • Do not charge more than the price you showed or the DRAP maximum retail price.

6. Customer information

Names, phone numbers and addresses customers send you, and the refill customer list you keep, may be used only to serve those customers. Send refill reminders only to customers who agreed to receive them. Do not sell, share or use this information for advertising, and delete a customer's details if they ask.

7. The "Verified" badge and sponsored listings

"Verified" means we checked basic details of your pharmacy. It is not a recommendation or a guarantee. Featured listings are shown as "Sponsored" while you have a paid plan.

8. When we remove or hide a listing

We may hide, suspend or remove a listing, with or without notice, if:

  • the pharmacy is not licensed, or we cannot verify it;
  • information, prices or stock are false or misleading, or prices are above the DRAP maximum retail price;
  • we receive repeated or serious complaints (for example reservations not honoured, overcharging);
  • the listing advertises medicines, breaks the law or breaks these terms;
  • a regulator or the law requires it.

Where reasonable we will tell you why and give you a chance to fix the problem. Plan payments are not refunded when a listing is removed for breaking these terms (see Refund & Cancellation).
