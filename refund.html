This policy covers payments for Sasta Dawa Finder business plans (pharmacy, clinic, NGO, company and API) made to Maverick Technologies, Islamabad. Payments for medicines you buy at a pharmacy are made to that pharmacy and are not covered here.

1. Free trial

New business accounts get a free trial of their plan (currently 30 days). The trial is free and needs no card or payment. Nothing is charged when it ends: paid features simply pause until you pay, and your data stays in your account.

2. How payment works

Plans are paid monthly, in advance, by JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank transfer (and online JazzCash where offered). After you pay, enter the transaction ID in Account → Plan & billing. We switch the plan on or extend it once we have checked the payment.

3. Cancelling

  • Plans do not renew automatically. We never take money from your account by ourselves.
  • To cancel, just do not pay for the next month. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for, then moves to the free plan.
  • You can delete your account at any time from your Profile.

4. When we refund

Monthly plans are not refunded for part of a month or for time you did not use, including when you cancel or delete your account during a paid month.

We do refund in full:

  • a duplicate payment (you paid twice for the same period by mistake);
  • a failed payment: money left your account but the payment did not reach us correctly, or we could not switch on the plan you paid for;
  • if we close your plan or stop a paid feature for reasons that are not your fault: the unused paid days.

Duplicate and failed payments must be reported within 7 days of the payment. We check the payment and send the refund to the same JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank account it came from, normally within 14 days of confirming it. We refund the full amount we received; charges taken by your bank or wallet provider may not be refundable by them.

No refund is given when a plan or listing is closed because the Terms of Service or Pharmacy Listing Terms were broken.

5. How to ask for a refund

Send us a message on WhatsApp or email with:

  1. the email address of your Sasta Dawa account;
  2. the transaction ID, date and amount of the payment;
  3. how you paid (JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank) and the number or account it came from;
  4. the reason (for example "paid twice").

We reply within 2 working days.
