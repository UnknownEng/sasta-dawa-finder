3 Simple Ways to Save on Medicines in Pakistan

🔬 1. Same Ingredient = Same Effect

Medicine brands approved in Pakistan with the same active ingredient work the exact same way. You are often paying extra only for the brand name.

🏷️ 2. Compare Price per Pill

Always check price per tablet or capsule, not the box price. Different boxes have 10, 20, 30, or 100 pills.

👨‍⚕️ 3. Ask Your Pharmacist

Simply ask your pharmacist: "Is there a reputable, cheaper local brand with this exact same formula?"
