What stays free

Searching medicines, prices, cheaper alternatives and My Medicines are free for everyone, with or without an account. When a business trial ends without payment, the account moves to the free plan: a pharmacy keeps one basic listing with phone and WhatsApp, and paid features pause until you renew.

Questions

How do I pay?
From your account's Plan & billing tab: send the amount by JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank transfer and enter the transaction ID. We check it and activate your plan, usually within a day.
Why does my pharmacy need verification?
So people can trust the listings. The Sasta Dawa team checks new pharmacies before they appear, and may call the number you give.
Where do the prices come from?
Medicine prices are the maximum retail prices published by the Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan (DRAP). Pharmacy prices are entered by each pharmacy.
Do you give medical advice?
No. We show prices and brands with the same formula. Patients should always ask their doctor or pharmacist before switching.
Can I cancel?
Plans are paid in advance by month or year and do not renew automatically. If you do not pay again, the account returns to the free plan.
